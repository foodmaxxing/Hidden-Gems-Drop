How It Works

Three things make a gem

Not every overlooked listing is worth your attention. The channel uses a scoring system built around three signals — the same instincts a seasoned eBay hunter would use, running automatically every thirty minutes.

01

Ending Soon

Auctions with hours left and a low current bid. Urgency and value in the same listing.

02

Low Competition

Listings that haven't attracted attention yet — few watchers, still wide open.

Every listing also passes a condition gate — only Good, Very Good, Excellent, or New items make it through. Nothing worn beyond what you'd happily put on a shelf or a wall.
